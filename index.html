Our story

From a small circle to a growing Qur’an family.

URWA began with five sisters who wanted the Qur’an to stay part of everyday life, not only Ramadan. In Shawwal 1442, twelve sisters gathered for the first halaqah. By Allah’s permission, that beginning grew into a wider family connected by the Book of Allah.
