The teams behind the work

Our departments

URWA’s four core departments work together to serve the organisation’s vision. Each team has its own responsibilities, goals and achievements to report.

HALAQAH MANAGEMENT

Halaqa Manager Team

Oversees the smooth running of URWA halaqahs and supports teachers and students so that learning remains organised, consistent and beneficial.

Vision

To build well-managed, welcoming and productive halaqahs where every student is supported in their journey with the Qur’an.

Goals

  • Coordinate halaqah schedules, class arrangements and communication.
  • Monitor attendance, student participation and class consistency.
  • Support Ablas and resolve day-to-day halaqah challenges.
  • Follow up on student progress and identify learners who need support.
  • Prepare regular reports on halaqah performance and needs.

Achievements to date

  • URWA has established regular halaqahs and structured learning levels as part of its growth.
  • Halaqah coordination and student attendance tracking have been part of the organisation’s working practices.
Next results to document: Number of active halaqahs, students served, attendance rate, teacher support actions and improvements in student retention or progress.
FINANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

Finance Team

Stewards URWA’s funds and financial records with honesty, transparency and responsibility.

Vision

To ensure URWA’s financial resources are managed with amanah, clear accountability and in support of the organisation’s mission.

Goals

  • Prepare and maintain budgets for programmes, events and operations.
  • Record income, expenses, contributions and outstanding payments accurately.
  • Keep receipts and supporting documents for every transaction.
  • Provide regular financial updates to the authorised leadership.
  • Promote responsible spending, financial planning and transparency.

Achievements to date

No verified Finance Team achievements have been provided for publication yet. This section is ready for the team to add completed work supported by financial records.

Next results to document: Budgets prepared, reports submitted, records reconciled, funds raised, costs managed and financial procedures introduced. Share only figures approved for publication.
MEDIA & COMMUNICATION

Social Media Team

Manages URWA’s online presence and communicates programmes, announcements and beneficial reminders to the wider community.

Vision

To build a consistent, engaging and trustworthy digital presence that reflects URWA’s values and connects more people to beneficial knowledge.

Goals

  • Plan and maintain a content calendar across URWA platforms.
  • Create clear, attractive and accurate posters, captions and videos.
  • Promote classes, events, enrolment and community activities.
  • Follow an approval process before publishing official content.
  • Monitor engagement, respond appropriately and report performance.

Achievements to date

  • A social-media workflow has been established, including content requests, drafting, approval, publishing, engagement monitoring and archiving.
  • URWA has used digital content to communicate programmes and community activities.
Next results to document: Campaigns completed, posts published, audience growth, reach and engagement, enquiries generated and consistency of the content calendar.
EVENTS & GATHERINGS

Event Team

Plans and coordinates URWA events and gatherings, ensuring that each activity is purposeful, organised and welcoming.

Vision

To create memorable and beneficial experiences that strengthen sisterhood, learning and connection within the URWA community.

Goals

  • Develop an annual events calendar and clear event objectives.
  • Coordinate venues, budgets, logistics, volunteers and programmes.
  • Work with the Social Media Team on publicity and announcements.
  • Manage registration, attendance and communication with participants.
  • Collect feedback and prepare a report after each event.

Achievements to date

  • URWA’s public journey records Day of Arafah activities and Eid celebrations.
  • Memorise and Rise and December retreats are also recorded among the 2023–2024 community activities.
Next results to document: Events delivered, attendance, participant feedback, volunteer involvement, budgets and lessons applied to future events.

How we work

From vision to measurable results

A shared process helps every department turn good intentions into clear action and accountable progress.

1. Set the vision

Agree on the purpose and the change the department exists to create.

2. Define goals

Set specific targets, owners, resources and realistic deadlines.

3. Deliver the work

Assign tasks, coordinate with other teams and document activities.

4. Review & report

Record what was achieved, what is ongoing and what needs support.

A living record of our amanah

Every goal deserves follow-through.

Department progress should be reviewed regularly, with completed achievements supported by dates, evidence and outcomes. The next step is to maintain each department’s goals and progress in one shared reporting system.
