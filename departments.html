Our shared direction

Every department moves URWA forward.

Our vision is to create meaningful connections with the Qur’an and its people, fostering growth and empowering a community that lives by its teachings. Our mission is to have the Qur’an recited in every Muslim home in Kenya, reaching homes around the globe.

One vision

Keep the Qur’an at the centre of our decisions, programmes and service.

Shared responsibility

Give every team clear responsibilities, goals and accountability.

Measurable growth

Review our work honestly, celebrate completed milestones and improve what remains.

The teams behind the work

Our departments

Each department has a distinct responsibility, while working in coordination with the wider URWA leadership and other teams.

LEARNING & TARBIYAH

Qur’an & Academic Department

Leads the academic direction and quality of Qur’an learning across URWA programmes.

Vision

A learning community that recites the Qur’an correctly, understands its guidance and puts its teachings into practice.

Goals

  • Provide structured learning pathways for different levels.
  • Maintain clear lesson plans, learning outcomes and class schedules.
  • Support Ablas with academic guidance and teaching resources.
  • Review learner progress and strengthen consistency in halaqahs.
Progress documented: URWA’s public journey records the development of structured learning levels and regular halaqahs. Department-specific completion figures have not yet been published.
MEMORISATION & REVISION

Hifdh & Muraja’ah Department

Supports learners who are memorising the Qur’an and protecting what they have already memorised.

Vision

To nurture strong, sincere and sustainable relationships with the Qur’an through memorisation, revision and reflection.

Goals

  • Develop realistic memorisation and muraja’ah pathways.
  • Encourage consistency, accurate recitation and revision habits.
  • Equip learners with progress plans and supportive follow-up.
  • Identify challenges early and guide learners towards manageable solutions.
Progress to record: Hifdh support is part of URWA’s programme direction. Specific numbers of students, juz completed or programme outcomes should be added from the department’s verified records.
TEACHER DEVELOPMENT

QTP & Teacher Development

Strengthens the people entrusted with teaching, mentoring and guiding learners.

Vision

A confident, prepared and compassionate body of teachers who deliver beneficial knowledge with excellence and good character.

Goals

  • Provide teacher development in lesson planning and classroom practice.
  • Promote consistency in teaching standards and learner care.
  • Support teacher induction, mentoring and continuous development.
  • Build a culture of reflection, feedback and improvement.
Progress documented: QTP-101 is listed in URWA’s programme offering as a teacher development programme covering character, lesson planning and teaching excellence.
COMMUNICATION & OUTREACH

Media & Communications

Communicates URWA’s work clearly, consistently and in a way that protects the trust of the community.

Vision

A recognisable and trustworthy URWA voice that shares beneficial knowledge, celebrates community work and reaches more homes.

Goals

  • Maintain a clear content calendar and consistent brand identity.
  • Coordinate event publicity and programme announcements with departments.
  • Use an approval process for posters, captions and public statements.
  • Review engagement and keep an organised archive of approved content.
Progress documented: A social-media workflow has previously been established, including advance requests, content drafting, approval, publishing, engagement monitoring and archiving.
SISTERHOOD & COMMUNITY

Events & Community

Creates opportunities for members to connect beyond lessons through beneficial gatherings and shared experiences.

Vision

A connected community where members feel welcomed, supported and inspired to grow in faith together.

Goals

  • Plan purposeful gatherings, retreats and community activities.
  • Coordinate event timelines, teams, communication and follow-up.
  • Encourage participation and belonging across the community.
  • Collect feedback and document lessons after each activity.
Progress documented: URWA’s public timeline records Day of Arafah activities, Eid celebrations, Memorise and Rise, and December retreats during 2023–2024.
GOVERNANCE & SUPPORT

Administration & Operations

Provides the coordination, records and operational support that allow URWA’s departments to work effectively.

Vision

A well-organised, accountable and dependable institution that fulfils its responsibilities with amanah.

Goals

  • Clarify team roles, reporting lines and departmental responsibilities.
  • Maintain accurate membership, attendance and organisational records.
  • Coordinate plans, resources, meetings and follow-up actions.
  • Track departmental targets, risks and completed work.
Progress documented: URWA’s public journey records the introduction of more structured teams in 2025. Internal operational outcomes should be updated by the administration team.
BROTHERS’ LEARNING

Brothers’ Section

Coordinates the brothers’ Qur’an learning activities as part of URWA’s wider mission.

Vision

To make accessible, consistent Qur’an learning available to brothers and support Qur’an-centred homes and communities.

Goals

  • Organise and support brothers’ halaqahs and learning schedules.
  • Coordinate teachers, learners and programme communication.
  • Maintain clear learning pathways and attendance records.
  • Strengthen collaboration with the wider URWA mission while respecting appropriate section arrangements.
Progress documented: Brothers’ halaqahs are recorded as part of URWA’s 2025 organisational development.

How we work

From vision to measurable results

A shared process helps every department turn good intentions into clear action and accountable progress.

1. Set the vision

Agree on the purpose and the change the department exists to create.

2. Define goals

Set specific targets, owners, resources and realistic deadlines.

3. Deliver the work

Assign tasks, coordinate with other teams and document activities.

4. Review & report

Record what was achieved, what is ongoing and what needs support.

A living record of our amanah

Every goal deserves follow-through.

Department progress should be reviewed regularly, with completed achievements supported by dates, evidence and outcomes. The next step is to maintain each department’s goals and progress in one shared reporting system.
